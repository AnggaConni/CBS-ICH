How to use this tool

A short guide for communities, bearers and practitioners.

What is this?

This is a notebook for your community. It helps you write down your own living heritage — what it is, who keeps it alive, how healthy it is, what threatens it, and what you plan to do about it.

The questions follow the themes used in international heritage reporting, but they are rewritten in plain words so that anyone can answer them.

Important to understand
  • This is not an official government or UNESCO form. It is a working tool for your community.
  • Your answers describe your own situation. They are not an official national report.
  • If a government office later asks for information, your saved record can be handed over as your community's own evidence.
  • Two themes in international reporting are counted only at world level, so they are not asked here.
Your data stays with you

Nothing is sent anywhere. Everything is stored inside this device's browser. You choose when to share, using your phone's normal sharing (Bluetooth, chat apps, email) or by exporting a file.

Please back up regularly. If you clear your browser data or uninstall the browser, records may be lost. Use "Back up all records" to keep a safe copy.

Before you record anything
  1. Ask the community first. Explain what the record is for and who may see it.
  2. Agree on what must not be written down, photographed or shared. Some knowledge is restricted by custom — respect that completely.
  3. Include everyone: elders and young people, women and men, quiet voices as well as loud ones.
  4. Read the record back to the community before you treat it as final.
The eight parts
1. Basics — name, type, place.
2. Permission — who agreed, and what must stay private.
3. The heritage — what it is and what it means.
4. Health check — how strong it is and what threatens it.
5. Our actions — what has been done and what is planned.
6. Self-check — 24 short themes, each scored 1 to 5.
7. Summary — strengths, weaknesses, priorities.
8. Sign-off — who checked and approved this record.
The 1-to-5 scoring
1
Nothing yet — this has not started at all.
2
Just beginning — something small has started, but it reaches few people.
3
Halfway — it is really happening, but it is uneven and nobody checks results.
4
Working well — it happens regularly, only small gaps remain, results are tracked.
5
Fully in place — it works, it lasts, and it is reviewed and improved regularly.
Sharing and exporting
Share — send the record through your phone's usual apps (Bluetooth, chat, email).
PDF / Print — opens the print window; choose "Save as PDF".
Spreadsheet (CSV) — opens in Excel or similar.
Word document — for editing and adding photos.
Data file (JSON) — the complete record; use this to move it to another device.
Catalogue file (XML) — a library-standard description, for archives and museums.
