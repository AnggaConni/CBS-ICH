Why this repository

Evidence built with communities, not just about them.

The recorder helps communities express their own perspectives through a structured, reusable record. Each update can be retained as a historical version while the latest report remains easy to discover.

◎ Community ownership

Capture consent, access conditions, community validation and the people entrusted with each record.

⌁ Structured documentation

Record heritage practices, bearers, transmission, viability, threats, safeguarding actions and future needs.

↺ Historical continuity

Keep earlier submissions as immutable snapshots, allowing change and safeguarding progress to be understood over time.

A simple workflow

From community conversation to national insight.

01 · DOCUMENT

Prepare a community record

Explain consent and access conditions, then complete the community recorder.

02 · REVIEW

Validate with knowledge holders

Read the record back together, correct sensitive details and agree what can be shared.

03 · CONTRIBUTE

Submit and retain history

Contribute up to two active records per community. New submissions create versions instead of erasing earlier evidence.
